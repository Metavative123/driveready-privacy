DriveReady Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Information We Collect

When you register and use DriveReady we collect: your name, email address, phone number, password (stored encrypted), optional driver/badge information, and any inspection records, photos, signatures, and documents you submit through the app.

How We Use Your Information

We use this data to create and secure your account, send OTP and password-recovery messages, run vehicle inspections and reminders, and generate reports and service notifications you request.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only when you trigger a feature that sends it — for example, emailing or exporting an inspection report to a recipient you choose. We may share data with service providers (e.g. cloud hosting, email/SMS delivery) strictly to operate the app, and only as required by law.

Data Retention

We retain account and inspection data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal/record-keeping obligations. You can request deletion at any time.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us. You may also delete your account from within the app's Settings screen where available.

Data Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted storage/transmission, access controls) to protect your data, though no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

DriveReady is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact: Drivereadyapp@hotmail.com
