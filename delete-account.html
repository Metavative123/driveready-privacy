Delete Your DriveReady Account or Data

Last updated: September 9, 2026

How to Request Account Deletion

To permanently delete your DriveReady account and all associated data:

  1. Open the DriveReady app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account (if available on your app version), or
  3. Send a deletion request by email to Drivereadyapp@hotmail.com from the email address registered on your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

We will process your request and confirm by email within 30 days.

How to Request Deletion of Specific Data (Without Deleting Your Account)

If you want specific data deleted (for example, a particular inspection report or photo) without deleting your whole account, email Drivereadyapp@hotmail.com describing what you'd like removed.

What Gets Deleted

Deleting your account removes your name, email, phone number, password, driver/badge information, and inspection records, photos, signatures, and documents associated with your account from our active systems.

What May Be Retained

We may retain limited information where required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or record-keeping obligations, or transaction records already processed by our payment providers (Stripe/RevenueCat), for a limited additional period after deletion.
